How a cohort starts

Two ways to enroll. Both end in the same first text. Live text-message enrollment is not enabled in this demonstration — this page is a walkthrough of the intended path.

Partner-led enrollment

  1. Start a cohort. Title, county, season, default product, channel (SMS).
  2. Add applicators. Name, mobile number, field label — one at a time or paste a list. For each: tick “This person agreed in writing to receive text reminders about their ESA plan.”
  3. First text. Each applicator receives: “AgriNexus Compliance: You're enrolled in the [cohort] ESA follow-through. Reply YES to receive plan reminders, STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”
  4. On YES. The row moves from Invited to Enrolled and a case can be planned.

Applicator-led enrollment

  1. At a training session. Short link or QR on a card or slide.
  2. Phone form. Name, mobile, county, product I plan to use.
  3. Consent. “We will text you about one thing: your ESA plan for this field, including reminders until you confirm what you did. Up to three texts per application. Reply STOP at any time to end them. Your number is shared only with the program running this cohort. We keep the case receipt for two years as this demonstration's retention policy.”
  4. Same first text, same YES. The case appears on the partner's board as Enrolled, no plan yet.

Enrollment is shown as a walkthrough. Live text-message enrollment is not enabled in this demonstration.
